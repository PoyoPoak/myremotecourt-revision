General Information / Remote Appearance Information

Remote Appearance

The Orange County Superior Court offers the opportunity to appear remotely in various case types. The public is encouraged to take advantage of remote appearance opportunities to reduce travel time, parking cost and potential hearing delays.

Please verify your remote appearance eligibility by following the eligibility wizard below or by calling.

Your hearing is a {{ caseData?.hearingType }}. According to California Code of Civil Procedure Section 367.5 and California Rules of Court, Rule 3.672, you are allowed to attend this hearing remotely via video appearance.

Remote appearances help reduce travel time, parking costs, and potential delays while ensuring you can participate fully in your court proceedings.

In-Person Appearance Required

Your hearing is a {{ caseData?.hearingType }}. For this type of hearing, you must appear physically at the court location.

Court trials require in-person attendance to ensure proper confrontation rights and the presentation of evidence. You must appear at the {{ caseData?.location }} on your scheduled hearing date: {{ caseData?.hearingDate }}.

Please arrive at least 30 minutes early and bring a valid photo ID.
